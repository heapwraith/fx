Every path, one dash away.

Folders open at once and files show up as you type. Pathdash is a native file manager for Windows 10 and 11, open source and built for the keyboard.

What it does

Everything you use in Explorer, with the same shortcuts, plus what it was missing.

Whole-disk search

Reads the NTFS MFT and answers as you type, with filters like type:image, size:>100mb and date:week.

Quick Look

Space previews images, text and highlighted code, Markdown, ZIP, PDF and Office without opening another app.

Safe operations

A copy queue, conflicts that never overwrite without asking, and Ctrl+Z that undoes any operation, even from another day.

Keyboard first

Ctrl+K finds any command or folder by name. Built-in terminal on Ctrl+J, dual pane on F3.

Organised

Tabs, colour tags, the total size of every folder, saved searches that keep themselves up to date, and a column view.

Can be the default

Folders, drives and Win+E open in Pathdash. "Back to Explorer" restores what was there.

Privacy

Pathdash sends nothing about you. The update check only reads the list of releases on GitHub; a crash report stays on your PC, with no paths or names, until you choose to send it. Both can be turned off in Settings.

Still in beta

Until code signing is active, Windows may warn about an "unknown publisher": click "More info", then "Run anyway". Updates arrive by themselves and apply when you close Pathdash. Found a problem? Open an issue.
